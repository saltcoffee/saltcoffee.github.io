Purro 隐私政策

生效日期 2026 年 11 月 25 日

本政策说明 Purro App 如何处理信息，涵盖设备上的互动和偏好、通过 Apple 进行的内购，以及你主动发送的支持邮件。

App 如何处理信息

使用 Purro 无需创建应用账户。抚摸和尾巴互动产生的触摸输入在设备上实时处理，用于模拟猫咪动作。呼噜声和触觉反馈也在设备上生成。Purro 不保存触摸记录，也不将触摸数据、使用行为或设备标识符发送给开发者。

Purro 不请求访问麦克风、相机、位置、通讯录或相册。App 不含广告、第三方分析或追踪 SDK，也不跨 App 或网站追踪你。

设备上的偏好和缓存

Purro 在设备上保存你选择的猫咪、体型、显示模式、语言、拟真手设置、引导状态，以及用于显示解锁状态的 Pro 权益缓存。App 也可能缓存渲染素材以加快加载。这些信息用于恢复偏好、显示正确的功能和加快加载，Purro 不将它们上传到开发者的服务器。

本地偏好会保留在 App 中，供下次打开时使用。选择系统中的「删除 App」可移除该安装的本地偏好和缓存。Apple 账户里的购买记录由 Apple 管理，删除 App 后仍可通过 Apple 恢复符合条件的购买。

购买与恢复 Purro Pro

Purro Pro 是可选的一次性内购，由 Apple App Store 处理付款和购买记录。Purro 使用 Apple 的 StoreKit 验证交易、恢复购买和更新解锁状态。开发者不会收到你的付款卡资料、账单地址或 Apple 账户密码。Apple 如何处理账户和交易信息，见 App Store 与隐私。

支持邮件与删除请求

如果你主动发送支持邮件，我们会收到你的发件地址、邮件正文和你附上的资料。通过 App 的「联系我们」撰写邮件时，邮件主题会预填 App 版本和构建号。邮件由所用的邮件服务传递，我们使用来信信息回复、排查问题和跟进请求。

支持邮件仅在处理和跟进请求或履行适用法律义务所需期间保留。你可以发邮件请求访问、更正或删除主动提供的个人信息，也可以要求我们停止后续跟进。

政策更新与联系

更新后的政策会发布在本页，并注明生效日期。若有隐私问题或数据请求，请联系 bltlovecs@163.com。

Purro Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

This policy explains how the Purro app handles information, including on-device interactions and preferences, purchases through Apple, and support emails you choose to send.

How the app handles information

You can use Purro without creating an app account. The app processes touch input from petting and tail interactions on your device in real time to animate the cat. It also generates purring and haptic feedback on your device. Purro does not keep touch histories or send touch data, usage activity, or device identifiers to the developer.

Purro does not request access to your microphone, camera, location, contacts, or photo library. The app has no ads or third-party analytics or tracking SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Local preferences and cache

Purro stores your selected cat, body proportions, display mode, language, virtual hand preference, and whether you have seen the gesture guide on your device. It also caches Pro entitlement status locally to display unlocked features and may cache rendering assets to speed up loading. Purro uses this information to restore your preferences and run the app without uploading it to the developer’s servers.

Your preferences stay in the app so it can use them the next time you open it. Choose Delete App in your device settings to remove the preferences and cache for that installation. Apple manages your purchase history, and eligible purchases remain restorable through Apple after you delete the app.

Buying and restoring Purro Pro

Purro Pro is an optional one-time in-app purchase. The Apple App Store handles payment and purchase records. Purro uses Apple’s StoreKit to verify transactions, restore purchases, and update access to Pro features. The developer does not receive your payment card details, billing address, or Apple Account password. For information about how Apple handles account and transaction information, see App Store & Privacy.

Support emails and deletion requests

If you email us for support, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to include. If you start the email from Contact Us in the app, Purro pre-fills the app version and build number in the subject. Email providers deliver these messages. We use the information you send to respond, troubleshoot, and follow up on your request.

We retain support emails only as long as needed to handle or follow up on the request or meet applicable legal obligations. You can email us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you provided, or to ask us to stop following up.

Policy updates and contact

We publish updates to this policy on this page and update the effective date. For privacy questions or data requests, contact bltlovecs@163.com.
